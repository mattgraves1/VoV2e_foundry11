A region map shows a stretch of the desert the Referee has made with the Region Generator. This page is the key to it.

Distance

Each hex is one day of travel. A route five hexes long takes five days.

What you can see

The map starts dark. The Party token shows where you are, and you see the hexes around it. Ground you have already crossed stays on the map, dimmed. The Referee moves the Party token as you travel.

The ground

The desert is blue. Lighter ground is higher: a lone mountain is pale at its peak and darker toward its foot, and canyons and dry riverbeds are dark channels. Thin lines mark each step in height, like a contour map.

Other marks

Vault. A diamond round the disc marks the way into a vault.
Landmark. A feature you can steer by from far off: a statue, a crater, a strange tree.
Route. A known way between two places. Routes appear when the Referee reveals them, and only where you have seen them.
Hazardous route. A route drawn in red has a danger on it.
