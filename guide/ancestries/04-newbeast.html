Newbeasts are animals engineered to walk and speak like people. What most shapes playing one is your animal form: it can help or hinder your saves, and it lets you talk to your own kind.

In the book

Beasthood gives you advantage on saves whenever your animal nature would plausibly help, and lets the Referee impose disadvantage when it would get in the way. Kinship lets you speak with any creature that shares your underlying animal form, even one that could not normally talk, though it need not like you (Vaults of Vaarn, page 13).

At character creation

  • The Spark Tables step has a Roll Animal Form button. It picks one of 80 forms, from New-Aardvark to New-Scarab, all equally likely. Roll it before moving on.
  • The same step rolls or lets you pick your name, hue, mask and quirk.
  • You are made a Biological creature.
  • Two reminders are added to the Referee's Active Effects board, named with your form: for example Beasthood (New-Cat) and Kinship (New-Cat). You don't see them.

On your sheet

  • The Description tab shows your Animal Form with your other rolled details, and lists Beasthood and Kinship under your ancestry.
  • Biological is ticked under Creature Types on the Description tab.
  • Newbeasts have no ancestry item and nothing is applied to your rolls automatically.

What you do

  • When the Referee grants advantage from Beasthood, Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) the ability's name on the Stats tab to roll the save with advantage.
  • When they impose disadvantage, Shift-click it instead.

What the Referee decides

  • Beasthood: whether your animal nature helps or hinders a given save. The Referee has a reminder naming your form. Say why your animal would help; they rule.
  • Kinship: which creatures share your form, whether they will talk, and how they feel about you.

Try it

  1. Make a Newbeast and click Roll Animal Form at the Spark Tables step.
  2. On the new sheet, open the Description tab and find your Animal Form, Beasthood and Kinship.
  3. On the Stats tab, Ctrl-click DEX and see the save roll with advantage, as it would when Beasthood applies.
