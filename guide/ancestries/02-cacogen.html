Cacogen are the mutated descendants of those left on the surface after the Collapse. What most shapes playing one is your mutations: you start with three, and can trade Levels for more.

In the book

Corrupted Blood gives you three d100 mutation rolls at creation; where two contradict, the later one wins. Proteus lets you take a new mutation instead of the usual HP and ability increases when you gain a Level, and gives you disadvantage on saves to resist mutation and other metamorphic effects (Vaults of Vaarn, page 9).

At character creation

  • The Spark Tables step has a Roll Mutations (d100 x3) button. You must roll it before the character can be created.
  • Clicking it again rerolls all three. A repeated number is rolled again, so your three are always different.
  • If two mutations both change your unarmed attack (such as two kinds of claws), the earlier one is marked Superseded and dropped with all its effects.
  • Each mutation becomes an item in your inventory, taking no slots. Its bonuses to abilities, HP, slots or armour are already added to your sheet.
  • A mutation that gives a natural weapon adds a weapon item, already equipped.
  • You are made a Biological creature.

On your sheet

  • Your mutations are in your inventory. Click a name to read it.
  • The Forgettable tab reminds you of mutation rules that are easy to miss.
  • The Description tab lists Corrupted Blood and Proteus under your ancestry.

What you do

  • Some mutations have a d20 icon titled use. Click it to use the mutation; chapter 5 of the Player's Guide covers these.
  • When you click LEVEL UP, the window has a This level choice: Three Abilities and 1d8 HP, or Proteus — roll another mutation instead. Pick Proteus and a new mutation is rolled for you, never one you already have. The choice is not offered past Level 10.
  • If you later lose that Level, the mutation it gave you goes with it.

What the Referee decides

  • The disadvantage on saves against mutation and metamorphic effects is not applied for you. Shift-click the save when the Referee says it applies.
  • Contradictions between mutations, other than two that both change your unarmed attack.
  • Any mutation effect the sheet does not track, as listed on the item or the Forgettable tab.

Try it

  1. Make a Cacogen and click Roll Mutations (d100 x3) at the Spark Tables step. Read the three results.
  2. On the new sheet, find the three mutation items and open the Forgettable tab.
  3. Click the right XP arrow once, then LEVEL UP, and look at the This level choice.
