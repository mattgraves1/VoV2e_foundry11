Cacklemaw are seven-foot hyena-headed women, and an exile is one too vile even for them. What most shapes playing one is that once a fight starts, you bite, you keep killing, and you struggle to stop.

In the book

No Quarter: you must pass an EGO save to spare a beaten foe or to retreat from a fight. Biter: when you hit with a melee attack, you may add d6 fang damage. Overkill: when a melee attack kills a foe, you may at once make another melee attack on a nearby target (Vaults of Vaarn, page 21).

At character creation

  • You get a Biter Fangs weapon item. It takes no slots and is already equipped.
  • You are made a Biological creature.

On your sheet

  • Biter Fangs shows a +1d6 badge instead of attack and damage icons. It is not an attack of its own.
  • That d6 is added to the damage roll of every melee weapon you use, your Unarmed Strike included.
  • The Description tab lists all three rules under your ancestry.
  • Every EGO save you roll from the Stats tab is followed by a No Quarter reminder in chat.
  • When your melee damage kills a targeted foe, chat names Overkill and says you may attack again. If one blow kills several, you get one reminder, not several.

What you do

  • Attack with your melee weapon as usual (chapter 3 of the Player's Guide). Target first: with nothing targeted, no kill is noticed and Overkill never appears.
  • When Overkill appears, target the next foe and attack again with the attack icon. Nothing is rolled for you.
  • When you want to spare a foe or flee, click EGO to roll the save.

What the Referee decides

  • When No Quarter applies, and what happens if you fail. The system only reminds you.
  • Whether the next target is near enough for Overkill.

Try it

  1. Make a Cacklemaw Exile and find Biter Fangs in your inventory. Hover its badge.
  2. Target a creature, attack with your Unarmed Strike, and on a hit click damage. The roll has an extra d6.
  3. Click EGO and read the No Quarter note that follows.
