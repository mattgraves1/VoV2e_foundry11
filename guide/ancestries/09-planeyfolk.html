Planeyfolk are flat, two-dimensional people born of hypergeometry gone wrong. What most shapes playing one is your gear: you cannot simply pick up a 3D object, so each new item has to be attuned before you can use it.

In the book

Flat: you are like a living painting. You slip through cracks and under doors, cannot be seen from the side, take half damage from bludgeoning, and double from slashing or piercing. Attune with Matter: holding a 3D object takes a DEX save, but an hour of quiet concentration attunes you with an item and adds it to your inventory (Vaults of Vaarn, page 23).

At character creation

  • Everything the creator gives you starts attuned, so your starting kit works at once.
  • You get two ancestry items, taking no slots: Flat and Attune with Matter. They have no use icon; click a name to read it.
  • You are made a Biological and Hypergeometric creature.

On your sheet

  • Any item you gain later is unattuned. Its row shows an hourglass icon titled Attune — begins an hour of quiet concentration.
  • An unattuned item is still carried, counted in your slots, and can be given or dropped. You just cannot equip it, attack with it or use it. Try, and a notice says why and suggests asking the Referee for a DEX save.
  • An item you already have equipped can be used, attuned or not.
  • Your own body parts, such as natural weapons and mutations, never need attuning.
  • When damage is applied to you, bludgeoning is halved and slashing or piercing doubled. Attacks with the Hypergeometric tag also deal you double.

What you do

  • Click the hourglass on the item's row. A notice says you have begun, and an Attuning row appears on the Active Effects board.
  • The hour passes as the Referee moves the Exploration Clock. When it is done, chat says you are attuned and the hourglass goes away.
  • If you need something now, ask the Referee for a DEX save. If they allow it, you can equip or use the item without attuning it. Put it down and you need another save.

What the Referee decides

  • The DEX save to hold an unattuned item, and whether to allow the equip or use.
  • Whether your hour of concentration is interrupted. They can pause or reset it on the board.
  • Slipping through cracks, and not being seen from the side.
  • How you eat, drink and sleep. The book does not say.

Try it

  1. Make a Planeyfolk and equip your weapon. It works: starting gear is attuned.
  2. Click Add item in your inventory. The new item's row shows the hourglass.
  3. Click the hourglass, then ask the Referee to move the clock an hour. Watch for the attuned message.
