Synths are thinking machines of metal and plastic. What most shapes playing one is that food and rest do not heal you: you repair yourself with Synth Parts.

In the book

Synthetic Flesh: you need not eat or breathe, shrug off suffocation, drowning, toxins, extreme temperatures and spores, take double damage from electrical weapons, and use the Synthetic Wounds table. Synthetic Mind: LogLang attacks can harm you, and magnetic fields deal d6 INT a round. Repairs: an hour and one Synth Part heals d8 + CON HP, or one Wound at full HP; rations heal nothing. Dead synthetics yield more parts on an INT save (Vaults of Vaarn, page 11).

At character creation

  • You get 3 Synth Parts and no food or water rations.
  • A Repairs item is added to your inventory. It takes no slots.
  • You are made a Synthetic creature.
  • A Synthetic Mind reminder is added to the Referee's Active Effects board. You don't see it.

On your sheet

  • The REST row shows Repairs instead of your water and food. Short and long rests restore no HP; at full HP a long rest can still heal a Wound or restore ability damage.
  • The TOX row reads Immune — Synthetic.
  • When damage is applied to you, electrical damage is doubled, and poison, spore, heat, cold, suffocation and drowning damage does nothing. Chat says which rule applied.
  • Wounds are rolled on the Synthetic Wounds table.
  • Elixirs are refused: the elixir is kept and a notice says why.
  • Saves against nanomachine infections are rolled with disadvantage.
  • Magnetic fields hurt you: d6 INT each round. The card of a Magneticrab, a Magnetic Orb, a Magnetic Stew drinker, Magnetised Palms or an Electromagnet hazard has a button that starts it on the targeted Synths. It shows on your Active Effects board and only ticks while a combat is running.

What you do

  • To repair, click the use icon on Repairs. Below full HP it spends one Synth Part and heals d8 + CON. At full HP it asks which Wound to fix, and spends the part only when you pick one. With no parts, or nothing to fix, nothing is spent. While Deprived it heals no HP.
  • To extract parts, click Extract Synth Parts on a dead synthetic creature's sheet, choose who rolls, and click Roll INT Save. The parts go to the Dropped Items box. One try per body. If you can't open the sheet, ask the Referee.

What the Referee decides

  • Synthetic Mind: LogLang attacks are the Referee's to apply.
  • When you leave a magnetic field, or it stops. The Referee removes its row from your board.
  • The hour a repair takes. The click heals at once; the Referee decides whether you have the time.
  • Disease: synthetics are immune unless noted, and the Referee is reminded of that when one is applied.

Try it

  1. Make a Synth and check the REST row and the three Synth Parts in your inventory.
  2. Lower your current HP, then click the use icon on Repairs. Watch HP rise and Synth Parts drop to 2.
  3. Click Short in the REST row and read why no HP comes back.
