Mycomorphs are fungus grown through a human corpse. What most shapes playing one is your spores: a once-a-day release that can put a room to sleep, turn it on itself, or poison it.

In the book

Twice Born lets you make INT saves to remember what your original body knew, even knowledge lost in the Collapse. Detritivore lets you eat rotten matter; a rotting meal doubles a Short Rest's healing, and you have advantage on saves against poison and toxins. Spores affect as many biological targets as your Level; you make a CON save when you release them, and on a failure you have none left that day (Vaults of Vaarn, page 17).

At character creation

  • The Spark Tables step has a Roll Spores (d20) button. Roll it: if you skip it, you get no Spores item.
  • You get three ancestry items, taking no slots: Twice Born (named after your corpse once you roll it at the Spark Tables step, such as Twice Born: Soldier), Spores (named after your spore type) and Detritivore.
  • You are made a Biological and Fungal creature.

On your sheet

  • Your three rules are in your inventory. Click a name to read it.
  • The TOX row shows ADV — Detritivore. Toxin Die saves roll with advantage for you.
  • An Out of spores for today box sits under the REST row. It is ticked for you when a spore save fails, and a Long Rest clears it.
  • As a Fungal creature, damage from ordinary physical attacks is halved and fire damage is doubled when it is applied to you.

What you do

  • Spores: target the creatures, then click the d20 icon (use) on the Spores item. It rolls your CON save and chat says how many targets you may affect. Each targeted creature gets a save card. Leeching Spores heal you by the damage they deal. While the box is ticked, the icon only says you have none left.
  • Twice Born: click its use icon when the Referee allows a recall. It rolls your INT save.
  • Detritivore: in the Short rest window, tick The meal is rotting to double the HP. It only works when you eat food, not water.

What the Referee decides

  • What your original body knew, and what a successful Twice Born save tells you.
  • Whether your meal really is rotting.
  • The limit of targets equal to your Level. Chat states it; nothing stops you targeting more.
  • When the day turns over. They can untick the spores box by hand.

Try it

  1. Make a Mycomorph and click Roll Spores (d20) at the Spark Tables step.
  2. Target a creature on the scene and click use on your Spores item. Read the save card, and check the spores box.
  3. Type a lower number into current HP, click Short, choose a food ration and tick the rotting box. The outcome line now says doubled by Detritivore. Click Rest.
