True-kin are the unmutated heirs of the sealed arcologies. What most shapes playing one is that the ancients' machines may take you for their master, or turn on you.

In the book

True-kin have two special rules. Pure of Blood gives you advantage on reaction and persuasion rolls with other true-kin, but only while you show no visible mutation. Inheritor lets you face pre-Collapse security systems and guard synths with an opposed EGO save: win and the machine serves you as its new master; lose and it becomes implacably hostile (Vaults of Vaarn, page 7).

At character creation

  • The Spark Tables step rolls your body, face, hair and attire, then your name, caste, manner and quirk.
  • You are made a Biological creature.
  • An Inheritor item is added to your inventory. It takes no slots.
  • A Pure of Blood reminder is added to the Referee's Active Effects board for your character. You don't see it.

On your sheet

  • Inheritor sits in your inventory with a d20 icon titled use. Click its name to read the rule.
  • The Description tab lists Pure of Blood under your ancestry, and shows your rolled caste, manner and quirk.
  • Biological is ticked under Creature Types on the Description tab.

What you do

  • When you meet a security system or guard synth, target its token if it has one, then click the use icon on Inheritor. It rolls your EGO save; Ctrl-click for advantage, Shift-click for disadvantage.
  • With a machine targeted, chat compares your total with 10 + the machine's EGO and says whether it serves you or turns on you.
  • With nothing targeted, chat shows your total and the Referee compares it. A natural 20 or natural 1 settles it either way.

What the Referee decides

  • Pure of Blood is not applied for you. The Referee grants the advantage when you deal with true-kin, and decides whether you are visibly mutated. Ask for it when it applies.
  • What a machine that serves you will actually do, and how a hostile one acts.
  • Whether a given machine counts as a pre-Collapse security system or guard synth.

Try it

  1. Make a True-kin with Create Character (Vaarn), choosing only True-kin at the Ancestry step.
  2. Find Inheritor in your inventory and click its use icon with nothing targeted. Read the chat card.
  3. Open the Description tab and find Pure of Blood and your caste.
