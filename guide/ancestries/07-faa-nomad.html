The Faa are blue-skinned desert wanderers, children of the goddess Vaa. What most shapes playing one is the desert itself: you go days without water, strike first from the sands, and can charm a sandworm.

In the book

Desert Metabolism: you only become Deprived by thirst after three days without water, and do not die of it for three weeks. Ambusher: in the blue desert you can make an opposed PSY save to ambush a hostile encounter, even one that has already ambushed your party. Worm Wise: make an EGO save to charm a sandworm; it lets you ride it briefly or helps you, but on a failure it attacks and hunts you through its territory (Vaults of Vaarn, page 19).

At character creation

  • The Starting Equipment step notes that your water lasts three days before Deprived. You still start with three water and three food rations.
  • You get two ancestry items, taking no slots: Ambusher and Worm Wise.
  • You are made a Biological creature.

On your sheet

  • Ambusher and Worm Wise are in your inventory, each with a d20 icon titled use.
  • A Long Rest with no water does not make you Deprived. Instead chat says your three days to Deprived begin, and a Faa Nomad without water row appears on the Active Effects board, counting the days.
  • The next Long Rest where you drink ends that count.
  • If you do become Deprived, the chat card and the board both say a Faa perishes of thirst after three weeks.

What you do

  • Ambusher: target the creature you want to ambush, then click use. It rolls your PSY save against 10 + that creature's PSY, and chat says whether the ambush springs. With nothing targeted, a notice asks you to target first.
  • Worm Wise: click use when you meet a sandworm. It rolls your EGO save, and chat says whether the worm is charmed or affronted.
  • To go without water, choose nothing for water in the Long rest window. As for anyone, a night without water gives no benefit from the rest.

What the Referee decides

  • Whether you are in the blue desert, and so may use Ambusher.
  • How the ambush round and the encounter play out.
  • What a charmed worm does for you, and how an affronted one attacks and tracks you.
  • When your three days are up. The Referee switches Deprived on; the count does not do it by itself.

Try it

  1. Make a Faa Nomad and find Ambusher and Worm Wise in your inventory.
  2. With a creature on the scene, click use on Ambusher once with nothing targeted, then again with it targeted. Compare what happens.
  3. Click Long, set water to nothing, rest, and open the Active Effects board to see your count.
