Neoblooms are plants grown wise and mobile. You heal by rooting in the sun, you burn easily, and your bloomboons give you strange powers.

In the book

Photosynthesis heals d8 + CON HP for each hour rooted in damp soil under real sunlight; three days without it and you perish. Flammable doubles damage from fire and heat, and a hit sets you burning for d8 a round until put out. Bloomboons: you start with one, rolled on a d20, and may roll another instead of HP and ability increases when you gain a Level; a repeat takes the next one down (Vaults of Vaarn, page 15).

At character creation

  • The Spark Tables step has a Roll Bloomboon (d20) button. Roll it: if you skip it, you get no bloomboon.
  • Three items are added, taking no slots: Photosynthesis, Flammable, and your bloomboon, named like Bloomboons: Glue Resin.
  • You are made a Biological creature.

On your sheet

  • Flammable has no button. When flame damage is applied to you it is doubled, and a Burning effect (d8 a round) is put on you. Chat says both.
  • If your bloomboon is Barbed Bark, it works on its own: an attacker who misses you in melee takes damage equal to your Level.

What you do

  • Photosynthesis: click its use icon for each hour rooted. It rolls d8 + CON and heals, up to your maximum. While Deprived it heals nothing.
  • Your bloomboon: click its use icon. What happens depends on the boon.
    • The three Pollens, Glue Resin, Oily Sap and Puppeteer Roots: target the creatures first. Save cards are posted for them.
    • Vampiric Roots: target the creature. The hold is put on it, with its escape save.
    • Blast Pods, Lashing Vines, Seed Cannon, Shield Vines and Tesla Bloom: the ability cost is paid and the part is added as an item. The leaf icon (Shed this part) removes it, and its cost becomes ordinary ability damage.
    • Medicinal and Toxic Fruit: d6 HP is spent and the fruit is added to your inventory.
    • Mirrored Leaves rolls your DEX save.
    • Others post a line for you and the Referee to resolve by hand.
  • At LEVEL UP, This level offers Bloomboons — roll another Boon instead. A repeat takes the next boon down. Not offered past Level 10.

What the Referee decides

  • Whether you have the hour, damp soil and real sunlight. The click heals at once.
  • The three days without photosynthesis are not tracked. Keep count with the Referee.
  • When the fire is out. The Referee applies each round's burning and removes it.
  • Once-a-day limits on bloomboons, and boons such as Grafting, Luftpods and Vantablossom.

Try it

  1. Make a Neobloom and click Roll Bloomboon (d20) at the Spark Tables step.
  2. Lower your current HP, then click the use icon on Photosynthesis.
  3. Click your bloomboon's use icon and read what chat says.
