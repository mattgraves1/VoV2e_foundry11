Lithlings are solemn, long-lived beings of living crystal from a distant star. What most shapes playing one is your HP: you start with a lot of it, but you can never heal a single point.

In the book

Crystalline Flesh: your base AV is 10 + your Level, up to 20. You need no food or drink. Fire, cold, poison, radiation, electricity, fungal spores and suffocation do you no harm, but bludgeoning deals you double. Inevitable: you roll 10d8 for your starting and maximum HP. You cannot heal HP by any means, and gain no maximum HP from Levels. At 0 HP you crumble to dust, leaving a small lithling seed (Vaults of Vaarn, page 25).

At character creation

  • The creator warns you about Lithling HP before you roll it.
  • The Hit Points step has a Roll 10d8 button. There is no option to take a flat 5 HP.
  • You get no food or water rations. You still get the chemcell torch and your rolled gear.
  • You are made a Mineral creature.

On your sheet

  • Your armour is 10 + your Level (at most 20), with any worn armour added on top. It rises with your Level.
  • The TOX row reads Immune — Mineral.
  • When damage is applied to you, flame, cold, electrical, toxin, fungal spore and suffocation damage are cancelled, and bludgeoning is doubled.
  • Heals the system would give you are refused, and chat names Inevitable as the reason.
  • At 0 HP no Wound is rolled. Chat says you crumble to dust, and (your name)'s Lithling Seed appears in your inventory.

What you do

  • Short and Long rests spend no rations and restore no HP.
  • A Long Rest still offers its other choice, even when you are hurt: heal one Wound, or restore damaged abilities by one point.
  • When you click LEVEL UP, the window has no HP roll: it says Inevitable: no maximum HP from Levels. From Level 10, a new Level grants nothing at all.

What the Referee decides

  • Radiation. There is no radiation damage in the system, so your immunity to it is theirs to apply.
  • What becomes of your seed.

Try it

  1. Make a Lithling and click Roll 10d8 at the Hit Points step. Note your AV on the Review step.
  2. On the sheet, type a lower number into current HP, then click Short and Rest. Chat says no HP is restored.
  3. Click Long and rest. See the choice of healing a Wound or restoring abilities.
