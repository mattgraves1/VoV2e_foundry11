Combat uses Foundry's Combat Tracker. The system handles initiative, applies damage and wounds, and posts a card each round listing the effects to resolve. Remember to target: every attack, save and effect acts on the tokens you have targeted.

Starting a fight

  1. On the active Scene, add the tokens to the Combat Tracker, the characters' as well as the enemies', and click Begin Combat. A character left out of the tracker gets no end-of-combat usage die roll. Players can add their own tokens: right-click the token and click its combat icon.
  2. Initiative is rolled for you, in whichever of the book's four ways you chose in Initiative system (chapter 1). Any initiative button re-rolls it for everyone. Chat says who acts first.
  3. Characters a player owns are the PC side. A character you made and haven't assigned to a player doesn't count; it is sided by its Disposition like anyone else. For everyone else, Friendly fights with the PCs, and Hostile, Neutral and Secret act with the enemies. In David initiative, Neutral and Secret don't count toward either side's size.

Ambushes and night watch

Click the ninja icon in the Combat Tracker (Spring an Ambush or Night Watch). Choose who springs it and who must save, then click Post roster. Each target gets a Roll PSY Save button: players roll their own, and you roll the creatures', all at once if you like. When every save is in, chat says whether the ambush springs. If it does, the ambushers attack with advantage this round.

A creature's sheet

Each attack or ability on a creature's sheet has small icons. Hover to see what each does. Target first for anything that affects someone else.

  • attack and damage: the same two clicks as a player's weapon.
  • Compel the target to save: one save card per targeted token. The target's player rolls it (chapter 4 of the Player's Guide), or you do.
  • Start this per-round loss on the targeted tokens: the first loss lands now, and later ones come from the round card.
  • Remind me about this each round: puts a line on every round card until you switch it off.
  • Roll its damage against the targeted tokens: for abilities that hit without an attack roll.
  • Use it on every other actor in the combat: for effects that reach the whole fight.
  • Guard the targeted token: a bodyguard. A blow that would kill the guarded token is held for you to decide.
  • Other icons heal, add temporary HP, cut maximum HP, spawn creatures or roll a stat, as the ability needs.

The creature's MORALE field and Check button are at the top of its sheet.

When a creature attacks a character

  1. Target the character's token.
  2. Click the creature's attack. Chat says hit or miss.
  3. Click damage. It goes onto the character: temporary HP first, then HP. Going below 0 applies a Wound automatically, and any save it calls for is posted.

Weapon effects that force saves or conditions post their cards to the target on a hit.

The round card

While any per-round effect is running, the system posts Round N — apply these: at the start of each round, listing them in turn order. With nothing running, there is no card. Each line says whose turn it resolves on. Lines about creatures are whispered to you; lines about player characters are public.

  • Apply … damage, Roll … and apply: the player whose character caused the effect can press these, or you can. For an effect a creature caused, only you can. Each works once per card.
  • Roll …: a reminder roll. Apply the result yourself.
  • HP changes, spawns and area saves: only you can press these.

Effects that run out are listed under Ended:.

Morale

Nothing checks morale for you. When the book calls for it, click Check on the creature's sheet. The card says whether it holds or flees, hides or parleys. Creatures that never flee, always flee or are your call say so instead of rolling.

Gambits

When an attack hits with a total over 20, a Gambit available card appears. Whoever made the attack chooses: Attempt a gambit, or Forgo damage, no Save. For a creature's attack, that is you. Forgoing damage is on the honour system; nothing stops the damage roll.

Fleeing and charging

  • Characters flee with the FLEE button on their own sheet. On a success they leave the tracker. On a failure they can drop items to try again.
  • Characters declare a charge with DECLARE CHARGE on their sheet.

Death

A creature brought to 0 HP is killed and chat says so, and it is marked defeated in the Combat Tracker, however it died. A heal that brings it back above 0 HP clears the mark.

Ending the fight

Click End Combat. The system rolls the usage die of every ranged weapon fired during the fight, clears effects that lasted a set number of rounds, and clears combat-only states such as a berserk frenzy.

Try it

  1. Drag two Bestiary creatures and a character onto the map, add them to the tracker, and begin combat.
  2. Target the character, then use a creature's attack and damage.
  3. Start a per-round effect, such as a Seeker of Eyeless Wisdom's Brain Burster on the character, then click Next Round and read the round card.
  4. Click Check on a creature's sheet to roll its morale.
