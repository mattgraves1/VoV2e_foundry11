The system announces a character's death in chat, but it doesn't lock the character or stop anyone using the sheet. What happens next is up to you and the player. A Fatality, General Systems Failure or Ego-Engine Destroyed death is also listed on the character's Wounds tab, so you can see how they died.

How characters die

Each of these posts a line in chat saying the character is dead, and why:

  • Fatality: HP reaches −20 (−19 or −20 for a Synth).
  • Death's Door: a character on the Death's Door wound takes any further damage.
  • No room for Wounds: a new Wound needs slots the character doesn't have.
  • An ability bonus falls below −10.
  • Maximum HP reaches 0.
  • A failed save against instant death, such as a neurotoxin.
  • Lithlings crumble to dust at 0 HP (see below).

At exactly 0 HP, other characters are knocked out and roll a CON save instead of dying.

To kill a character deliberately, use Inflict a Wound on their Wounds tab. It lists every row of their Wounds table, the deadly ones included.

Things that stop death

  • Immortality Injector: while it lasts, the death line becomes "would have died … but the Immortality Injector holds them together". HP and Wounds still apply.
  • Look Out Sire: a bodyguard creature can guard one token (chapter 2). When a blow would kill the guarded token, the blow is held and a card is whispered to you. Click Name dies instead to have the bodyguard take it, or Let it land.

Bringing someone back

Run Resurrect Character from the Vaarn Macros. Choose the dead character and a route. Each route checks that it suits the character, and posts a card explaining what happened.

  • Mycomorph Spores (Biological): the character is reborn as a Mycomorph within d4 days, with the same sheet and items. An INT save decides whether they remember who they were; on a failure they drop to Level 1.
  • Necrotech (Biological): Phoenix Core and Lazarus implants bring them back as partly Synthetic, on the same sheet.
  • Pseudo-Womb clone (Biological): the same sheet. A failed CON save adds two new mutations.
  • Returning as a Spirit (anyone): roll d20 + Level against 16. On a success, a new Spirit of … actor is made for the player (see below).
  • Ego-Engine Transplant (Synth, unless the death was Ego-Engine Destroyed): a new Level 1 body with the same name and player. STR, DEX, CON and HP are rerolled; INT, PSY, EGO, personality and Gifts are kept. Items and wounds stay on the old sheet, so give across whatever the party recovered.

For the three routes that bring the character back on the same sheet (Spores, Necrotech and Pseudo-Womb), the macro also offers three clean-ups:

  • Remove all wounds, and with them anything a wound caused, such as Vischip Disabled's blindness or the daily fading of Deathblight.
  • Heal all ability damage, including a Severed Limb's STR and DEX.
  • End everything on their Active Effects board: afflictions, conditions, a Toxin Die, curses and the rest. Your GM reminders stay.

All three are ticked for Spores and Pseudo-Womb; for Necrotech only ability damage is. Change them as you see fit. The card lists what was removed. Lost maximum HP is not restored.

Nothing is charged. Track any cost or waiting time yourself.

Spirits

A returned spirit is a new actor owned by the same player, with the character's Level, maximum HP and abilities. It is incorporeal. Its sheet has three abilities:

  • Manipulate Object, which costs d6 HP.
  • Possess a single targeted creature, which costs d6 + the creature's Level in HP.
  • Long Rest, which restores full HP and needs no rations.

At 0 HP it fades away and can return at the next sunrise.

Lithlings

A Lithling at 0 HP crumbles into dust and leaves a seed item in their inventory. Whether and how the seed grows again is up to you; the system doesn't track it.

Creatures

A creature at 0 HP is killed and chat says so, and it is marked defeated in the Combat Tracker, however it died. Effects that last until their source dies end when it does.

Try it

  1. Make a test character with Create Character (Vaarn), of any ancestry but Synth (Pseudo-Womb needs a biological character, and only the creator sets that), and, on its Wounds tab, use Inflict a Wound to apply its Fatality row. Read the chat line.
  2. Run Resurrect Character, pick that character and Pseudo-Womb clone, and read the card.
