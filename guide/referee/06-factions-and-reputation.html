The book's factions are built in, with their goals, allies, enemies, NPCs and how to gain reputation with them. Each character tracks their reputation (REP) with every faction on their sheet.

Reading about a faction

Click Factions of Vaarn (the flag) in the Token controls. Pick a faction from the list to read its entry: summary, goals, allies and enemies, what happens if it triumphs, its NPCs, and its Gaining Reputation table.

An NPC with a paw icon is in the Bestiary. Click it to create that NPC in the world and open its sheet.

This window is for you only. There is no button to show an entry to the players; read out or paraphrase what they learn.

Your own factions

In Configure Settings:

  • Edit Factions: tick Hide on book factions you aren't using, and add your own under Your own. Only the name is required; fill in as much of the rest as you like. A hidden faction stops being offered anywhere, but a character who already has REP with it keeps that row.
  • Edit Relationships: set who counts as an ally or an enemy. Your changes are shown in bold; Reset to the book undoes them all.

The book's own text is never changed. Your additions are stored separately.

Reputation on the character sheet

The Reputation tab lists every faction, starting at Neutral (0). Each row shows the standing, from Nemesis (−10) to Hero (10), what that standing means, and how to gain REP.

  • + gains 1 REP. Every faction that is an enemy of it loses the same amount, and chat lists the changes.
  • − loses 1 REP. A loss never moves any other faction.
  • You can also type a number. REP stays between −10 and 10.

The character's player can change their own REP as well as you. Agree how REP is earned at your table.

Spending REP and reactions

REP can be treated as a currency; the tab lists the book's examples. There are no prices, so agree what it buys and lower the number by hand.

Roll a creature's reaction with the Reaction button on its sheet. Pick the character doing the talking, whether they can communicate (which adds their EGO), and the creature's faction, if it has one. The character's standing with that faction then counts: Liked rolls with advantage, Disliked with disadvantage, and the other standings show their book text on the card. Only you see the result. With no faction picked, REP doesn't change the roll.

Try it

  1. Open Factions of Vaarn and read a faction's entry.
  2. On a character's Reputation tab, click + for that faction and see which enemies lose REP.
  3. In Edit Factions, add a faction with just a name and save. It appears on the Reputation tab.
