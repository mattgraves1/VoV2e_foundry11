The Active Effects board lists everything that is running: timed conditions, afflictions, sustained Gifts, curses, upkeep, efforts, darkness and your own reminders. It is how you see what is going on and end things when they stop.

Opening it

Click Active Effects (the checklist icon) in the Token controls, which show only while a Scene is shown. Players have this tool too, on the active Scene.

  • You see everything, with every control.
  • Players see the effects on their own characters in full. Effects from creatures, curses and your reminders are hidden from them unless you choose to show them.

The top shows the time, and the combat round during a fight. Your +1 Turn, +1 Hour, +8 Hours and +1 Day buttons move the same clock as the Exploration Clock.

Reading the list

Everything is one list, sorted by character. Each row says who it is on, what it is, and how long it has left or has been running. Some rows show until switched off or No printed end; those stay until you end them.

The ✕ on a row ends it. Its tooltip says what that means for that row, such as End this now, Let go — stop sustaining this Gift, Upkeep met — stop counting, Lifted — the Daemon is satisfied or Abandon this effort.

Showing something to the players

A hidden row has an eye icon. Click it, type what the players should be told (for example Blinded: Reid), and click Show the players. They see only your words, not the effect's name or how long it has left. Click the eye again to hide it.

Starting things

  • Begin an effort: something that takes time, like forcing a door. Choose who, what, how long, and whether an interruption pauses or resets it. The row shows progress. When it finishes, chat says Effort complete and the row stays until you dismiss it.
  • Start an affliction: choose who is exposed and to what. An exposure card asks for the CON save, and your Infect button gives them the affliction if they fail. The affliction's row then offers its onset roll, a treatment where one takes time, and Cured.
  • Track a lapse: count how long someone goes without upkeep, such as a steed without food. The row quotes what the book says happens. End it when the upkeep is met.
  • Inflict a curse: a Quantum Daemon's curse on a character. For Jinxed, only you see the unlucky number.
  • Add a GM reminder: a note to yourself on any character or creature.
  • Put the party in darkness / End the darkness: adds or removes an In Darkness row on every character a player owns, and the Exploration Clock stops Search while it lasts.

Other rows arrive on their own: conditions from failed saves, elixirs and creature abilities, sustained Gifts, and reminders for items and companions.

When things end

  • Effects measured in time end when you advance the clock past them. Effects measured in rounds end at the right round. Chat lists them under Ended:.
  • Ending combat clears the effects that were given a number of rounds.
  • Afflictions, sustained Gifts, lapses, curses, your own reminders, Deprived, darkness, finished efforts and anything until switched off stay until you end them.

Try it

  1. Open the board and click Add a GM reminder on a character. See the row appear.
  2. Click Begin an effort for 3 Exploration Turns, then +1 Turn three times and watch it complete.
  3. End both with their ✕.
