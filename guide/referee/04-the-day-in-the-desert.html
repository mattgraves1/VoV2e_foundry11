Desert travel runs a day at a time. Each day starts with weather, an encounter check and the Vigilance Die, and ends with a Long Rest where the party eats and drinks.

Starting the day

Open the Exploration Clock, set Where to Desert, and click Start the day. In order, it:

  1. Rolls the weather. The players see today's weather and its rule. You also get a whispered forecast of what tomorrow can bring.
  2. Rolls the day's encounter check on the Desert Exploration Encounters table, using the clock's encounter die. The result is whispered to you.
  3. Offers the Vigilance Die. A public card lets the party roll it (see below).
  4. Brings in the day's catch from creatures that provide food, straight into their owners' inventories.
  5. Spoils perishable food that has kept overnight, and lists it.
  6. Tells you which retainers withered overnight. Remove those from the map yourself.

Start the day doesn't move the clock or use rations. Move time with +1 day, Push — double distance or Camp a day and night (chapter 3).

The weather

Click Desert Weather (cloud and sun) in the Token controls to see today's weather, its rule, tomorrow's odds and the last few days. From here you can also:

  • Roll the day's weather on its own, without the rest of Start the day.
  • Forecast: post tomorrow's odds again, whispered to you.
  • Choose a weather and click Set for today to override the chart for one day. The next roll ends it.
  • Untick Announce the weather to the players to keep the weather to yourself.
  • Back to the centre: restart the weather chart for a new journey.

Weather rules are shown, not applied. Apply half speed, no travel, extra water and the like yourself. The one exception is that Hazy and Dust Storm make the Vigilance Die roll at disadvantage.

The Vigilance Die

Anyone can click Roll the Vigilance Die on the day's card. On a 6 the party will spot their next encounter first; on a 1 the encounter spots them. Anything else has no effect. Apply the result yourself when the encounter comes.

Night watch

When something approaches the camp at night:

  1. Put the creature and the party's tokens in the Combat Tracker.
  2. Click the ninja icon (Spring an Ambush or Night Watch), choose the ambusher, tick Night watch, and pick who is On watch.
  3. Click Post roster. The watcher rolls their PSY save from the card.

Chat says whether the watch raises the alarm or is caught out. Remind the watcher to tick Stood watch in their own Long Rest window.

Rations

Players eat and drink in their own Long rest (Player's Guide, chapter 6). The window shows what each character and companion will use and what they get from the rest.

  • With no food there is no benefit from the rest. With no water, a character also becomes Deprived.
  • Characters with special diets are offered their own food first.
  • A companion that isn't fed doesn't heal, and after a few days it leaves: pets and steeds after 7 days, followers and mercenaries after 3.

A long rest also refreshes daily uses and clears a codex muddle.

By hand

  • Advance the day.
  • Apply the weather's effects and the Vigilance result.
  • Remove withered retainers.
  • Run night encounters with the ambush icon.
  • Make sure everyone takes their Long Rest.

Try it

  1. Open the Exploration Clock, set Where to Desert, and click Start the day. Read the three cards.
  2. Click Roll the Vigilance Die.
  3. Open Desert Weather and click Forecast.
