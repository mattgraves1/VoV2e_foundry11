Characters can gather followers, mercenaries, pets and steeds, and ride or crew vehicles. Companions are ordinary creature sheets with an owner.

Getting a companion

  • Followers and mercenaries: run Generate Follower or Mercenary from the Vaarn Macros. Choose the table and the recruiting character, and click Recruit. The rolls use that character's EGO. The new companion goes in a Generated Followers or Generated Mercenaries folder, already belonging to the character, and a summary is whispered to you.
  • Pets and steeds: drag one from the Pets or Steeds compendium, then set its owner (below) from its sheet in the Actors tab, not from its token on the map; a token of a compendium creature is a separate copy, and an owner set there isn't seen by the rest window.

Mercenaries expect payment after each expedition. The system doesn't track it; handle it at the table.

Who it belongs to

At the top of every creature's sheet, BELONGS TO shows its owner and kind. Click it to choose the character and whether it is a Pet, Steed, Follower or Mercenary. Only you can set it.

The owner and kind decide the companion level limit, whether it can level up, whether it eats at its owner's Long Rest, and whether it can receive XP. Setting an owner also gives the players of that character Foundry ownership of the companion, so they can open its sheet, drag its token out and run it, and makes its token linked and Friendly for every token placed from then on. A token already on the map stays as it was; replace it. Changing or clearing the owner takes back only the ownership that setting it gave.

Levels and the EGO limit

  • Pets, followers and mercenaries each have a pool: the total Levels of each kind a character keeps can't exceed their EGO. Steeds don't count. Going over asks you to confirm.
  • Pets and followers can gain Levels. A player gives them XP with their own XP arrow, which asks who the point goes to (Player's Guide, chapter 7). The companion's sheet then has its own LEVEL UP button. A pet also chooses better damage, morale or armour.
  • Mercenaries and steeds don't level.

Companions have a MORALE field and a Check button, like any creature (chapter 2).

Feeding them

Companions eat at their owner's Long rest. The rest window lists each one with a gets food and water box. An unfed companion doesn't heal, and leaves after a few days without food: followers and mercenaries after 3, pets and steeds after 7. Chat warns how long is left. When one leaves, it no longer belongs to the character.

Steeds

There are no riding rules in the system. Move the rider and mount together, and apply the book's rules yourself. A steed eats one food ration a day.

Vehicles

Drag a vehicle from the Vehicles compendium. Its sheet shows:

  • HULL: a vehicle loses 1 Hull for every 10 damage from a single attack. Less than 10 does nothing. Repair 1 Hull is a day's work with spare parts; advance the clock first.
  • SPEED and a Speed Save button, for pursuits and races. Compare the rolls yourself: beat 10 + the other vehicle's Speed.
  • ITEM SLOTS: the vehicle's inventory is its cargo hold.
  • CREW: drag characters from the Actors list into the Pilot and Gunner slots. A crew member's player gets control of the vehicle; clearing the slot takes it back. The vehicle's weapons fire using the crew member's DEX, and chat says so if a seat it needs is empty.

Creature, steed and vehicle sheets also show an approximate trade value (chapter 10).

Try it

  1. Run Generate Follower or Mercenary for a character and open the new follower's sheet.
  2. Drag a steed from the Steeds compendium and set its BELONGS TO.
  3. Take a Long Rest on the character and see both companions in the window.
