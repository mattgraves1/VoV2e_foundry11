Vaarn trades by barter, and so does the system. There is no currency and no buy or sell button. Items carry a value; you settle deals and move the items.

What things are worth

  • Every item's sheet shows its Trade Value at the top. Tags such as Bejewelled or Ancient count, and Advanced weapons are worth double. On a weapon made since 0.3.0, the box holds the base weapon's value and the line under it shows the worth with its tags (In effect: 3); that line is the value. On an older weapon the tags are already in the number.
  • Some tags are worth more to particular buyers. The sheet then adds a line such as … to Faa Nomads.
  • Exotica show an XP Value instead. They are traded for experience, not goods (Player's Guide, chapter 7).
  • Creature, steed, pet and vehicle sheets show an Approximate Trade Value, worked out from their Level or Hull.

Making a trade

  • Drag an item from one sheet to another to move it. For a stack, you are asked how many. Players can move items only between sheets they own, and into Dropped Items; a trade between two players' characters needs you.
  • Give to another actor (the ⇄ icon, Referee only) moves any item, with a dialog to choose who gets it.
  • Chat records each move.

Remove what the party trades away, and add what they get, by hand.

Trade goods

Generate Trade Good makes a trade good in the Items directory. Its Trade Value is per lot, and its Quantity is the number of lots.

Each good also has a secret quality that only you see, in a GM — Quality box on its sheet. The box says what the quality does and whether it is already in the numbers; a better or worse price is not, so apply it when the good is sold.

A False good has a Sold — start the … -day clock button. Use it when the party sells one, and the Active Effects board counts down to when the buyer finds out.

Settlements

Generate Settlement whispers you a settlement: its size, people, government and faith, its assets, buildings and landmark, and its Local Value Fluctuations:

  • Prized goods sell for twice their value.
  • Undesirable goods sell for half.
  • Despised goods can't even be given away.

Item sheets keep showing the base value; apply these yourself while the party is there.

Identifying Exotica

Players see an unknown Exotica as Unidentified Exotica. On its row you have two icons:

  • Ask for an INT save to work out what it does: choose who tries. On a pass it is identified; on a failure it must be appraised.
  • Identify it (appraised): for when the party pays for an appraisal at a settlement. It is identified at once.

Either way, chat tells everyone what it is.

Try it

  1. Run Generate Settlement and read the fluctuations.
  2. Run Generate Trade Good, open the new item, and read its GM Quality box.
  3. Drag it onto a character's sheet.
