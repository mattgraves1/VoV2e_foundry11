The Vaarn Macros compendium holds the system's generators: macros that roll on the book's tables and make the result. Only you can see them.

Running a macro

Open the Compendium Packs tab and then Vaarn Macros. Drag a macro onto your hotbar to keep it to hand, or right-click it and choose Import Entry. Either way you get a copy in your Macro Directory, and a copy is not updated when the system is: after an update, drag or import it again.

The compendium is refreshed from the system every time the world loads, so changes you make inside it are lost. To change a macro for your table, import it first (Import Entry) and edit your copy; your copies are never touched.

No generator needs a token selected. Where one acts on a character, it asks which one.

Where the results go

  • Items appear in the Items directory, not on anyone. Drag them onto a character's sheet.
  • Creatures and NPCs appear in the Actors directory, in a folder such as Generated Creatures or Generated NPCs.
  • Everything else is a chat card whispered to you.

Gear and treasure

  • Generate Weapon: choose the quality and, if you like, the weapon and tags.
  • Generate Armour, Generate Starting Gear.
  • Generate Advanced Exotica, Generate Advanced Implant, Generate Starting Implant.
  • Generate Gift, Generate Mutation, Generate Drug, Generate Crucible.
  • Create Codex Item: one of the book's equations, all twenty, or your own.
  • Generate Flavor Item: fine clothes, instruments, books, art, jewellery and the like.
  • Generate Trade Good: see chapter 10.
  • Generate Treasure Cache: choose the kind and size. It makes a container in the Actors directory, stocked with the loot, that players can't see until you give them access.

Mystic Gift effects

Every Gift starts with no effect set, however it was made. When you and the player agree what it does, set it on the Gift's Effects tab: damage of a type, healing, a condition (Blind, Entangled or a named effect) or prose. A Gift can have several. The tab suggests effects for the 20 sample Gifts and for every Quality. They are not from the book. The Vaarn Items compendium has the 20 sample Gifts in a Mystic Gifts folder, ready to drag onto a sheet. A world whose Items compendium was built before this has no such folder; empty the compendium and reload to get it.

Things you give to a character

These ask which character, and put the result straight on their sheet:

  • Alchemy Brewer: an elixir or an antidote.
  • Generate Flora: a plant from the book.
  • Generate Poison: build a poison, and optionally apply it to a character at once. They roll the CON save.
  • Grant Graft: a graft for a character with the Grafting bloomboon.
  • Generate Autarch Figment: one of the Jigsaw Autarch's four figments.

Creatures and people

  • Generate Monster: a new creature, of a type you choose or at random.
  • Generate NPC: a person of a chosen ancestry and level, with a Bestiary stat block if one fits.
  • Generate Rival Adventurer: a rival party member of a chosen ancestry and level.
  • Generate Quantum Daemon: a lesser or greater daemon.
  • Generate Follower or Mercenary: see chapter 9.
  • Generate Companion: despite its name, this makes the people of a place (a bandit camp, a Faa camp, a Hegemony outpost, a sanctum) or a minor faction's leader.

Places and stories

  • Generate Settlement: see chapter 10.
  • Generate Lair Rooms: rooms for a lair at a chosen depth, with their creatures.
  • Generate Vault: a whole vault, written to a journal with a page per room, an encounter table per level and, if you like, a hex Scene per level. See chapter 8.
  • Generate Room Contents: vault rooms and what they hold, for a room outside a generated vault. Each room rolls a d12 for its type (uninhabited, lair, treasure, lair and treasure, or special), or you choose the type, and then the book's rolls for that type, creatures, treasure and a special room's follow-up included. Set Rooms to fill several at once. Name a journal and each room is written to it as a page, with hazard buttons on the page (chapter 3).
  • Generate Narrative: story seeds, oases, petty gods and more, as a chat card.

The rest

  • Open Character Creator: the same as the Create Character (Vaarn) button.
  • Resurrect Character: see chapter 11.

Try it

  1. Drag Generate Weapon onto your hotbar and run it. Find the weapon in the Items directory.
  2. Run Generate Settlement and read the whispered card.
  3. Run Generate Monster and find it in Generated Creatures.
