The book's items are a starting point. This chapter shows how to make new ones, and how to change a book item so it fits your game. Everything here is done from the item's own sheet, and only you can do it: players can read what an item does, but cannot change it.

Two ways to set up an item

  • Make it. In the Items directory, click Create Item and pick its kind: a melee or ranged weapon, armour, an Exotica, a basic item, a mutation, an implant, a Gift, and so on. The kind matters. It decides which effects the item can have, and whether it is installed (part of the body, like a mutation), equipped (worn or in hand, like a weapon) or simply carried.
  • Plain numbers such as Damage, Slots and Trade Value can be typed straight into the boxes at the top of the sheet. That is all a plain item needs.
  • Anything that does something goes on the Effects tab: a save on a hit, ADV on a roll, healing when used, light. A rule of thumb: if you would otherwise have to remember it at the table, put it on the Effects tab.

The Effects tab

Every item sheet has one. It lists what the item does, one line each, such as While installed: +1 AV or On a hit: target DEX save or Blind for 1 round(s).

  • Add effect opens the builder. The pencil on a line edits it, and the bin removes it.
  • A weapon's tags show as their own lines, marked from the Heavy tag. You can remove one, but not edit it.
  • A book item's own rules show the same way: no pencil, but a bin. You can remove them and add your own beside them (see the Hushboots example below).

Building an effect: when, then what

The builder asks two questions. When does it happen? Then, Do: what happens? It only offers combinations the system actually carries out, so anything in the lists works. The When list depends on the kind of item:

  • When used gives the item's row on the character sheet a wand icon (use an effect). It can deal damage, heal, apply a condition or post a reminder. Damage and healing post a card with an Apply button, as other rolls do.
  • Always, while in its state works for as long as the item is installed, equipped or carried. You pick which. It can change AV, shed light, give an immunity, give ADV or DIS on saves or when fleeing, give ADV to escape a hold, double ration upkeep, stop the item breaking or corroding, or put a note on the Forgettable Effects tab.
  • When its attack hits (weapons): a save against a condition or death, ability damage, stripping the target's armour, extra dice against an AV band, adding an ability to damage, healing the wielder, or a note.
  • When its attack is rolled (weapons): roll to hit with another ability, ignore armour, break on a low natural roll, or refuse to attack underwater.
  • Weapons also get when an attack misses its wielder, when the wielder kills, when it is drawn and at a rest. Any item can work on a reaction roll.
  • Its stats is covered in the next section.

Depending on what you pick, the builder may ask for more:

  • Target: nobody named (the table decides), the user, one targeted creature, or up to a number of them.
  • Lasts: rounds, Exploration Turns, hours, days, or until you end it. The effect goes on the Active Effects board and ends itself.
  • Cost: a die of HP the user pays, from d4 to d20.
  • Name and Its words: the button's label, and the text shown on cards and reminders. Write the words as your players should read them.
  • On the Forgettable Effects tab (notes only): Always Active or On-Demand, and Benefit or Detriment.

If the builder cannot say what you want, use a note or a reminder. It does nothing by itself, but it puts your words in front of the player at the right moment.

Only if: conditions on an effect

Many effects have an Only if list. Add a row to make the effect wait for something. Tick not to reverse a row: not in daylight. There are three sorts of condition:

  • Worked out by the system, with no question asked: a combat is running; an x-in-N chance; the target is of a creature type (biological, synthetic, psychic and so on); the target is a player character; the target has a named state; the target wears metal armour.
  • Asked of you, once: in daylight under open sky, the weather, the terrain, warm-blooded. You are asked the first time and your answer is kept for the scene (daylight) or for that creature. You are not asked again every roll.
  • Asked each time, because it changes from moment to moment: in darkness, sneaking or hiding, charging, has moved this round, asleep, underwater, has eyes, has a brain, is an object, is submerged. The player is asked about their own character, and you are asked about a target.

Always-on effects take only the conditions nobody needs to be asked about: a combat is running, daylight, the weather and the terrain.

Some effects carry a narrower condition of their own:

  • Extra damage dice against an AV band adds dice when the target's AV is at most, or at least, a number: +1 die against AV 13 or less for a weapon that punishes the unarmoured.
  • ADV or DIS on reaction rolls can be limited to followers of the religion that blessed or cursed the item. You are asked whether the creatures met are followers.
  • A hit's save can be limited to player characters only, non-player characters only, or biological targets only.

Stats: changing the numbers

Pick Its stats to give an item a usage die; set its slots or the hands it takes; multiply its trade value, for everyone or only for one buyer (Faa Nomads); or make it metal or not. A weapon can have its damage dice set, increased or multiplied. Armour can have its own AV changed, or be set to be worn as body armour, a helm, a face piece or a shield.

The box at the top of the sheet keeps the base number, and a line underneath shows what is in effect: In effect: 2d8 — an effect changes it. Edit the base and the result follows. Weapons made by the system since 0.3.0 work this way too, so a Heavy dagger shows 1d6 in the box and In effect: 2d6 underneath. Weapons made before 0.3.0 keep their old numbers in the box and show no In effect line. That is expected.

Equipping and hands

Any carried item can be made something you hold: a lamp in one hand, a staff in two. Give it Set the hands it takes, or any effect that works while equipped. It then gets the equip toggle on the character sheet and takes those hands when equipped. Equipping is refused if the hands are not free.

Mystic Gifts

A Gift's Effects tab works the same way, but a Gift has its own four kinds of effect, and they follow the Gift rules:

  • Gift damage and Gift healing roll the die the user chose to pay, plus their PSY. You pick the damage type: kinetic, beam, blast, flame, electrical or TOX.
  • Gift condition: Blind or Entangled, or a named effect of your own, which goes on the Active Effects board as a reminder. Gifts always hit, so there is no save, and the condition lasts until you end it.
  • Gift described effect: your words only. The HP cost is still paid.

A Gift can have several effects. When it is used, the player picks which one, or Other use for anything else. The book's sample Gifts, and Gifts made by Generate Gift, also show Suggestions on their Effects tab. These are not from the book, just ideas to start from. Add one and change anything. A Gift you name yourself has none, so build its effects from the list above.

Worked examples

A new weapon: the Glare-Glaive. Create a melee weapon. Type 1d6 into Damage, then add:

  • Its stats: Set the hands it takes, 2.
  • Its stats: More (or fewer) damage dice, 1. The sheet now reads In effect: 2d6.
  • When its attack hits: Target saves or takes a condition, DEX, Blind, 1 round, biological targets only.
  • When its attack hits: Extra damage dice against an AV band, 1 extra die, AV at most 12.

A new mutation: Lichen Skin. Create a mutation. It is installed, so its effects work as long as the character has it. Add:

  • Always: Change the bearer's AV, 1.
  • Always: ADV or DIS on saves, ADV, on saves vs poisons and TOX.
  • When used: Heal, 1d6, target the user, only if in daylight. Name it Bask. The mutation's row gets the wand icon. Using it asks you whether it is daylight, then posts the healing.

A new gadget: the Storm Lamp. Create an Exotica. Add:

  • Its stats: Give it a usage die, Ud6.
  • Its stats: Set the hands it takes, 1.
  • Always, while equipped: Shed light, in a colour you choose.
  • When used: Deal damage, 1d6 electrical, one targeted creature. Name it Discharge, and write its words: roll the usage die after.

Changing a book item: Hushboots. In your game, they also help the wearer run. Open the Vaarn Items compendium and drag Hushboots into the Items directory. Change that copy, never the compendium's. Its Effects tab already shows its book rules (ADV when sneaking or attacking Blind creatures). Add:

  • Always, while carried: ADV or DIS when fleeing, ADV.
  • Its stats: Multiply its trade value, 2, only to Faa Nomads.

Keep the book name until you have added an effect. A book item finds its rules by its name, so a copy renamed before it is edited loses them. Once you have added one effect, the book rules are copied onto the item, and you can rename it freely: Hushboots of Flight.

A Gift as well: Mind Lance. Create a Gift and add Gift damage, blast. Then add Gift condition, a named effect, Reeling, so the player can choose either one when they use it.

Try it

  • Make the Glare-Glaive and give it to a test character. Check that the Effects tab lists all four lines and that the sheet shows In effect: 2d6. Equip it (it takes both hands), target a creature, and attack. A hit posts a Roll the DEX save card for the target. The damage roll adds the extra d6 if the target's AV is 12 or less.
  • Make the Storm Lamp. Try to equip it while the Glaive is equipped: it is refused, because both hands are full. Unequip the Glaive, equip the Lamp, and watch the token light up. Use Discharge on a target.
  • Delete the test character and the items when you are done.
