The character creator rolls everything the book asks for and builds a ready-to-play character sheet at the end.

Opening it

Go to the Actors tab of the sidebar and click Create Character (Vaarn) at the top.

The Referee must be logged in when you click Create Character at the end, because their client makes the character for you. You own it once it's made.

The steps

A strip across the top shows where you are: Attributes, Ancestry, Spark, Hit Points, Equipment, Boon, Review. Each step has a button to go forward and a ← Back button. Every roll is posted to chat.

  1. Roll Attributes. Click Roll Attributes. You may swap two abilities once: click one ability, then the other. Re-roll All starts over.
  2. Choose Ancestry. Tick the ancestries you are willing to play (all are ticked to start), then click Roll / Assign Ancestry.
  3. Spark Tables. Roll or pick your appearance and personality. Some ancestries get an extra roll here, such as mutations, a bloomboon or an animal form. Do those before moving on.
  4. Hit Points. Click Roll HP. If the Referee allows it, tick GM option: offer 5 HP flat and choose Take 5 instead.
  5. Starting Equipment. Choose Melee or Ranged and click Roll Weapon, then Roll Armour and Roll Gear. The next step opens once all three are rolled.
  6. Starting Boon. Roll a d6, or choose if the Referee allows it. Some boons have a second roll (an implant, a gift, an exotica and so on). Click it before moving on.
  7. Review. Check the summary, type a Character Name, and (if you are the Referee) pick the player in Assign to Player. Click Create Character.

What you get

A new character sheet opens with your abilities, HP, ancestry rules, weapon, armour, gear, rations, a chemcell torch, and any mutations or boon items. Only your Unarmed Strike starts equipped; see the next chapter for equipping the rest.

If something goes wrong

  • No Create Character button on the last step: a step was skipped. The page lists which ones must be done.
  • "Roll mutations … before creating the character": go back to Spark Tables and do the mutation roll.
  • A chat note says your gear is over the slot maximum: the character was still made. Drop items until you fit (dropping needs an active scene), or ask the Referee to move some to another sheet.

Try it

Open the creator and go straight through, accepting every roll. At Review, name the character and click Create Character. The sheet that opens is yours to use in the next three chapters.
