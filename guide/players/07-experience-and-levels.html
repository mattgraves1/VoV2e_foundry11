In Vaarn you earn experience by trading away Exotica: each one traded is worth XP. You spend XP to gain Levels.

Recording XP

The XP count is on the Stats tab, between two arrows. Nothing adds XP for you; you record it by hand.

  • The right arrow (Trade an Exotica: +1 XP) adds 1. Click it once for each point you earn.
  • The left arrow (Lose 1 XP) takes 1 away, if you make a mistake.

An Exotica's sheet shows its XP Value. Most are worth 1; some are worth more, or less, because of their tags.

If you have a pet or follower that can level, the right arrow asks who gets the point: The experience goes to. Pick yourself or the companion and click Award.

Gaining a Level

A Level costs XP equal to your current Level. When you have enough, the LEVEL UP button next to your Level lights up. Its tooltip tells you the cost, or how much you still need. XP left over after paying is kept.

  1. Click LEVEL UP.
  2. Choose three different abilities to raise by 1 each. Abilities already at +10 are not offered.
  3. Leave Roll 1d8 for HP ticked to raise your maximum HP.
  4. Click Level Up.

Chat shows your new Level and every change. If you chose fewer than three abilities, or unticked the HP roll, you are asked to confirm first.

Cacogen and Neobloom characters get one more choice at each Level: take the usual three abilities and HP, or roll a new mutation (Cacogen) or bloomboon (Neobloom) instead.

From Level 10 on, each new Level gives only +1 maximum HP.

Losing a Level

Some creatures and some wounds take Levels away. The Referee handles this. A lost Level undoes exactly what that Level gave you: the ability points, the HP, any mutation it rolled. The XP you spent on it comes back. You can't drop below Level 1.

Companions

A pet or follower you command has its own LEVEL UP button on its sheet, and pays for Levels the same way. When the Referee makes it yours, you can open its sheet and drag its token onto the scene. The total Levels of your companions of each kind are limited by your EGO. Going over asks you to confirm.

Gleam

GLEAM on the Stats tab is your Gleam score. It is shown for reference; the Referee rolls the Gleam Test.

Try it

  1. On your character, click the right XP arrow once. You now have 1 XP.
  2. At Level 1 that is enough: click LEVEL UP, choose three abilities, and click Level Up.
  3. Read the chat card and check your sheet.
