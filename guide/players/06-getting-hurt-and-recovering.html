Damage comes off your HP. Going below 0 gives you a Wound, and resting is how you heal. The system does the bookkeeping; this chapter says where to look.

Hit points

  • HP is on the Stats tab: current and maximum.
  • TEMP is temporary HP. Damage uses it up first, and chat says how much it absorbed.
  • At exactly 0 HP you are knocked out. A save card appears; roll it as in chapter 4.
  • Below 0 you take a Wound. At −20, or in a few other deadly situations, chat says your character is dead.

If your character has Analgesia, your HP shows as ?. The Referee tracks it for you.

Wounds

When damage takes you below 0, the Wound is applied for you. Chat says which one and what it did: lower maximum HP, ability damage, damaged armour, or a Wound item that fills inventory slots.

  • The Wounds tab lists them. A Wound that a rest can't heal says so, and why.
  • Ability damage shows in the WOUND column beside each ability. It lowers the bonus you roll with.
  • The trash icon (Heal (remove) this Wound) removes a Wound once it has healed. Use it when a rest or the Referee says so.

Resting

The REST row on the Stats tab has Short and Long buttons, and shows the water and food you carry.

  • Short rest: eat or drink one ration and regain d8 + CON bonus HP. The window shows the outcome before you click Rest. With no ration, no rest is taken.
  • Long rest: a full night. Choose the food and water to use; the window shows what you'll get. With food and water you regain all lost HP. You can also mark that you stood watch.
  • At full HP, a long rest instead lets you heal one Wound, or restore damaged abilities by one point. A window asks which.

A long rest also clears a codex muddle and refills your daily uses.

Deprived

Going without food or water can leave you Deprived. It shows as a STATE row on the Stats tab. While Deprived you can't heal lost HP or benefit from rests; the rest still uses your rations. The Referee switches it off when it ends.

Exhaustion

Pushing on while travelling can exhaust you. Exhaustion appears as an item that fills an inventory slot. The Referee removes it when you camp in one place for a full day and night.

The Toxin Die

Poison gives you a Toxin Die, shown in the TOX row.

  • Save makes the CON save against the toxin. Pass and it is gone.
  • Roll rolls the die. The result comes off your HP. Roll it each round before you act in combat, or each exploration turn otherwise.
  • On a 1 or 2 the die gets smaller. When the smallest die rolls 1 or 2, you are cured.

Rest does not clear a Toxin Die.

Afflictions

When you are exposed to a disease or curse, a card asks you to Roll the save. The Referee decides what a failure means, and tracks the affliction's course and cure.

Try it

  1. On your character, type a lower number into current HP to take some damage.
  2. Click Short in the REST row. Read the outcome line, then click Rest and watch HP come back.
  3. Click Long and see what a full night would restore.
