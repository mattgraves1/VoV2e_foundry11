An attack is two clicks on the weapon's row in your inventory: attack (the d20 icon), then damage (the crossed swords). Target first, so the system knows who you are hitting.

You can only target tokens on the scene the Referee has made active, and your own token should be on it too, or you may see nothing. To put it there, drag your character from the Actors tab onto the scene; the Referee must be logged in, because their client places it. To take it off again, click it and press Delete. Your token sees only what is lit, so in the dark carry a light. Damage to a creature you don't own is applied by the Referee's client, so the Referee must be logged in; if not, a notice says the damage wasn't applied.

Step by step

  1. Equip the weapon. An unequipped weapon won't roll. Chat says it is not equipped.
  2. Target the enemy. Hover over their token and press T, or use the target tool on the left toolbar. You can target several tokens.
  3. Click the attack icon. Melee weapons roll STR, ranged weapons roll DEX, and psionic weapons roll PSY. Ctrl-click for advantage, Shift-click for disadvantage.
  4. Read the result. For each target, chat says whether you hit or missed. You hit if your total is higher than the target's armour.
  5. If you hit, click the damage icon. The damage goes onto every target you just hit. Click it once only; a second click deals the damage again.

With nothing targeted, the attack still rolls, but there is no hit or miss and no damage is applied. Tell the Referee your total instead.

Natural 20s and big rolls

  • A natural 20 always hits, whatever the armour, and your next damage roll is doubled. Chat says so.
  • A total over 20 that hits also offers you a gambit. A card appears in chat to choose one.

Natural 1s

On a natural 1 your weapon is dropped or jams, and chat says so. Some weapons are worse:

  • Fragile weapons break and need repair. Clear the Broken box on the weapon's sheet once repaired.
  • Delicate weapons break on a 1 or a 2.
  • Crystalline weapons shatter and are gone. Unstable ones explode in your hands.
  • Strong and Indestructible weapons shrug it off.

Ranged weapons and ammunition

You don't count shots. During a fight the weapon is marked as fired, and its usage die is rolled once when combat ends. That only happens if your character is in the combat tracker, so ask the Referee to add you when a fight starts. Outside a fight it rolls each time you shoot. An Exotica weapon rolls its die every time you use it, fight or not. When the die runs out, chat says the weapon is out of ammo. You can't make ranged attacks while blinded.

Joining a fight

When the Referee starts a combat, they may add you to it. If not, right-click your token and click the combat icon (crossed swords) to join. Your character needs to be in the fight for its turn, and for a ranged weapon's ammunition to be counted when the fight ends.

Fighting unarmed

Every character has an Unarmed Strike in their inventory. Attack with it like any weapon. Mutations such as claws replace it.

Charging

Next to MOVEMENT on the Stats tab, click DECLARE CHARGE before a charge. It clears itself when you roll melee damage.

Try it

  1. Ask the Referee to activate a scene and put a creature from the Bestiary on it, for example a Hagfluke. Drag your character from the Actors tab onto the same scene.
  2. Equip your weapon, target the creature with T, and click the attack icon.
  3. If you hit, click the damage icon and watch the creature's HP drop.
  4. Now clear your target (press T again) and attack. See that chat gives no hit or miss.
