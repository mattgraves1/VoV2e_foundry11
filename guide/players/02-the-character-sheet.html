The sheet has tabs on the left and your inventory on the right. The inventory is always shown, whichever tab is open.

The tabs

  • Stats: level and XP, HP, armour, the six abilities, rests, the Toxin Die, movement, and the combat buttons.
  • Description: your ancestry, appearance and personality, and a space for notes.
  • Wounds: any wounds you are carrying.
  • Forgettable: small rules from your items and mutations that are easy to forget. Always Active ones apply all the time; On-Demand ones you use when you choose to.
  • Reputation: your standing with the factions of Vaarn.

Rolling a save

On the Stats tab, click the ability's name (STR, DEX, CON, INT, PSY or EGO). It rolls a d20 plus that ability's bonus.

  • Ctrl-click (Cmd-click on a Mac) rolls with advantage.
  • Shift-click rolls with disadvantage.
  • If you are carrying too much, STR, DEX and CON saves get disadvantage on their own.

Item slots

The top of the inventory reads Slots used/limit — Hands used/max.

  • Your limit is 10 + your CON bonus.
  • Go over it and you are encumbered. The number turns red.
  • 20 slots is the absolute maximum. At 20 the header says (CAPPED) and nothing more can be added.

Equipping

Click the checkbox icon on an item's row. The sheet refuses if you lack free hands or the STR for it, and says why in a notice.

Usage dice

Items that run out, like ammunition, have a usage die. Items that never run out, like a chemcell torch, have none and show no use icon. Click the d20 icon on the item's row (its tooltip says use) to roll it. On a 1 or 2 the die shrinks one size. When a d4 rolls 1 or 2, the item is used up and its name greys out.

A weapon's usage die, such as a bow's, has no use icon: it rolls for you when you attack. During a combat it rolls only once, when the combat ends (chapter 3).

To see or set the die, click the item's name to open its sheet.

Dropping and giving items

  • Drop on the ground (the hand icon) moves the item to the Dropped Items box, marked with the active scene. Chat says what you dropped. Anyone can pick it up from there. It needs an active scene: with none, you get a notice and nothing moves, so ask the Referee to activate one.
  • Give to another actor and Delete Item are Referee-only. Ask the Referee to move or remove an item.

Try it

  1. On the character you made in chapter 1, equip your weapon and armour. Watch the armour number change.
  2. Click DEX to roll a DEX save. Then Ctrl-click it and see two d20s in the chat card.
  3. If any of your items has a usage die, click its use icon and read the result in chat.
  4. With a scene active, drop an item, then open the Dropped Items actor in the Actors tab to find it.
