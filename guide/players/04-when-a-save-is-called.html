A save is a d20 plus an ability bonus. You pass if the total is over 15. A natural 20 always passes and a natural 1 always fails.

Saves you roll yourself

When the Referee says "make a DEX save", click DEX on the Stats tab of your sheet. Ctrl-click for advantage, Shift-click for disadvantage. Chat shows your total; you and the Referee compare it with 15.

Saves that come to you on a card

When a creature's ability or a weapon's effect forces a save, a card appears in chat. It says who used what, and which save you must make, for example must DEX save vs blinded.

  1. Find the card with your character's name on it. Each target gets their own card.
  2. Click its button, for example Roll the DEX save. Only you (or the Referee) can press it for your character.
  3. The button is replaced by the result: your roll against 15, and passed or failed.

You don't choose advantage or disadvantage on a card. The card works it out from your items and conditions and says where each one came from.

What happens when you fail

The effect is applied for you. A condition such as Blind goes onto your character with its duration, and damage, ability loss or a higher Toxin Die land on your sheet. Chat says what happened.

A few results are left to the Referee. The card says so when that happens.

Weapons that call for saves

Some weapons force a save when they hit, like a blinding or entangling weapon. When you hit with one, the save card goes to your target automatically. You don't need to do anything else.

Try it

  1. Drag your character onto the active scene, then ask the Referee to target your token and use a creature ability that forces a save.
  2. Find the card in chat with your name and click Roll the … save.
  3. If you fail, look at your sheet to see what changed.
