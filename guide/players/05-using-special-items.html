Mutations, gifts, codices, implants, exotica and elixirs each work a little differently. Their controls are small icons at the start of the item's row in your inventory. Hover over an icon to see what it does.

Every item's sheet has an Effects tab listing what the item does, one line each. You can read it; only the Referee can change it. A line under a number at the top of the sheet, such as In effect: 2d6, means an effect changes that number, and the line is the one that counts. The box keeps the item's base number.

Mutations

  • Most mutations are passive. They have no button; their effect is already on your sheet, or listed on the Forgettable tab to remind you.
  • Mutations you activate, like Ink Ducts or Gas Glands, have a d20 icon titled use. Click it and chat says what happens. If the mutation affects someone, target them first.
  • A mutation with limited uses a day shows (N left) after its name. The hourglass icon (refresh) fills it back up at the start of a new day.
  • Natural weapons such as a beak or claws appear as weapons in your inventory. Attack with them as in chapter 3. They are always ready, so they have no equip box.
  • Horns, tusks, antlers and the like are added to your weapon's damage instead of being attacks of their own. Their row says so.

Mystic Gifts

  1. Target the creatures the Gift is aimed at.
  2. Click the Gift's use icon.
  3. If the Gift has effects set (see below), pick which one you're using. Other use is for anything else the table agrees on the spot.
  4. Choose the HP cost die. To damage or heal, pick the effect you want: the die you pay is the die you roll, plus PSY (pay a d10, deal d10 + PSY). For any other effect, pick by the targets' combined Level: 1–2 d6, 3–4 d8, 5–6 d10, 7–8 d12, 9 or more d20. Putting nine Level 1 creatures to sleep costs a d20. The Referee has the final say; the baseline is a d6.
  5. Tick Sustained if you want to hold the Gift. You pay the same die again every exploration turn it stays up.

Chat shows the HP cost, which is taken from your HP, and the effect roll (1 die + PSY). If you targeted anyone, a card offers Apply as damage or Apply as healing. Only you or the Referee can press it, and only once. A Gift can't heal its own user.

To stop sustaining a Gift, tell the Referee. They end it on the Active Effects board.

Giving a Gift an effect

The book only gives a Gift a name. You and the Referee agree what it does. Once you have, the Referee writes it on the Gift's Effects tab. Each entry is one way to use the Gift:

  • Damage, of a damage type such as flame. The card offers only that damage.
  • Healing. The card offers only healing.
  • Condition: Blind, Entangled, or a named effect such as Asleep. No effect die is rolled, and the targets don't get a save. The Referee applies it, and it lasts until they end it. The cost die is picked for you from the targets' combined Level.
  • Prose: describe it in your own words. You pay the cost and the text goes to chat.

For the book's Gifts, the Referee sees suggestions to start from. They are ideas, not rules. A Gift with no effects works exactly as above.

Hypergeometric Codices

Click a codex's read icon to try one of its equations. It is an INT roll against 15.

  • Pass: the equation works and chat describes it. Some post a damage card or a save card for your targets, so target first.
  • Fail: you are muddled and can't read any equations until a Long Rest. A natural 1 also causes a mishap.

Cybernetic Implants

An implant you already have may have a use icon if it does something when activated. New implants usually arrive in a sealed capsule. Click the capsule's install icon. If the body slot it needs is already taken, you get a warning and keep the capsule.

Exotica

  • An unknown Exotica shows as Unidentified Exotica and can't be used. Ask the Referee: they can call for an INT save to work it out, or you can have it appraised at a settlement. If you want to try it anyway, ask the Referee: they can use it from your sheet.
  • Exotica with charges or a usage die have a use icon. When they run out, they are removed from your inventory. A few only work while a combat is running, such as the Fate Invertor and those that grant armour until combat ends; the Berserker StimRig and Berserker Brew need a combat too.
  • Attuning (Planeyfolk only): a Planeyfolk must attune ANY new item, not only Exotica, before equipping or using it. Click the Attune icon. It completes after an hour of game time, which passes only when the Referee advances the Exploration Clock. Items from character creation start attuned.
  • Trading an Exotica away earns XP. See chapter 7.

Elixirs

Click an elixir's use icon to drink it. Synthetic and Mineral characters can't drink elixirs; the elixir is kept and a notice says why. A few elixirs, such as those that give you what their original owner had, are drunk by the Referee from your sheet: your click posts a note asking them to, and the vial is kept until they do.

Try it

  1. Open your Forgettable tab and read what your items and mutations remind you of.
  2. If you have a Mystic Gift, target a creature and click its use icon. Pick d6 and watch the HP cost come off your HP.
  3. If you have a codex, click read and see whether the equation works.
